Connecting a system

Applies to Verrandi. Effective 6 September 2026.

Connections are made from inside your own workspace, not from this page. Verrandi is set up for each business individually, and your workspace has its own address.

Connecting or reconnecting

Open your workspace, go to your connected sources, and choose the system you want to connect. You will be sent to that provider — Intuit, in the case of QuickBooks Online — to authorize the connection, and returned to your workspace when it is done. Reconnecting is the same route; a new authorization replaces the old one.

What you are agreeing to

Connecting a system is a standing grant, not a one-off. Verrandi will read that system on a schedule, including when nobody is signed in, until you disconnect it. The Privacy Policy sets out what is read, what is stored, and who can retrieve it.

If you do not have your workspace address

Write to alex@verrandi.com and we will send it to you. Verrandi is operated for a small number of businesses and there is no self-service sign-up today.

Disconnecting

You can disconnect at any time, from either end. See how to disconnect.
