Verrandi

Sacramento, California.

Verrandi reads the systems a business already runs on — email, documents, calendar, accounting — and helps the people running it work with what is there.

It answers questions from the firm's own records, surfaces what is worth attention, and prepares changes for someone to approve. It does not change anything in a connected system on its own initiative.

Verrandi is early software, operated today for a small number of businesses. There is no self-service sign-up. To ask about it, write to alex@verrandi.com.
